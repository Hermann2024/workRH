# WorkRH Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026-05-14

These terms define the baseline commercial and operational rules for customers using WorkRH.

## Service

WorkRH provides a SaaS platform for HR administration, telework tracking, leave, sickness, reporting, support and subscription management.

## Customer Responsibilities

The customer is responsible for:

- ensuring authorised use by its users;
- configuring accurate tenant, employee and compliance data;
- validating tax, labour law and social security decisions with qualified advisors;
- keeping credentials and internal processes secure;
- obtaining required employee notices and legal bases.

## WorkRH Responsibilities

WorkRH is responsible for:

- providing the subscribed services according to the active plan;
- maintaining tenant isolation and role-based access controls;
- operating reasonable security, backup and incident processes;
- notifying customers of material incidents according to the incident runbook;
- supporting export and deletion requests where technically available.

## Plans and Billing

Subscription plans define included seats, features and support level. Stripe is the payment provider for self-service plans. Enterprise terms may be agreed separately.

## Acceptable Use

Customers must not:

- attempt to access another tenant;
- reverse engineer, disrupt or overload the service;
- upload unlawful, malicious or unnecessary sensitive data;
- bypass subscription, security or rate controls.

## Availability and Support

Operational targets, response times and escalation routes are documented in the production and incident runbooks. SLA-backed support applies only where included in the plan or contract.

## Data Protection

The Data Processing Addendum applies when WorkRH processes personal data on behalf of the customer.

## Liability

WorkRH provides tooling and operational support. It does not provide legal, tax, accounting or employment advice.

## Termination

The customer may terminate according to the active plan or contract. WorkRH should provide reasonable export and deletion support before final data deletion.

This document is a production template and must be reviewed by legal counsel before public launch.
