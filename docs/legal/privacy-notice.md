# WorkRH Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2026-05-14

WorkRH processes HR, telework, leave, sickness and subscription data to provide the WorkRH SaaS platform to companies and their authorised users.

## Controller and Processor Roles

For employee HR data, the customer company is generally the data controller and WorkRH acts as processor.

For WorkRH account, billing, support, security and commercial data, WorkRH may act as controller.

## Data Categories

WorkRH may process:

- account identity data: name, email, role, tenant, authentication metadata;
- HR workspace data: employee profile, contract metadata, leave, sickness and telework declarations;
- compliance data: country, threshold calculations, audit trail and evidence references;
- billing data: subscription plan, seats, Stripe customer and payment status references;
- support data: tickets, diagnostics, page context and resolution notes;
- technical data: logs, security events, IP-derived request metadata and browser details.

## Purposes

Data is processed to:

- provide tenant-isolated HR services;
- manage subscriptions, invoices and plan entitlements;
- support telework compliance tracking;
- provide customer support and incident handling;
- secure the platform and prevent unauthorised access;
- maintain auditability and operational continuity.

## Retention

Retention periods must be configured with each customer according to their legal and HR obligations. WorkRH production operations should apply documented retention and deletion processes.

## Subprocessors

Potential subprocessors include hosting providers, email/SMS providers, payment providers such as Stripe, monitoring providers and backup infrastructure. The active list must be maintained before go-live.

## International Transfers

Where data is transferred outside the EEA, appropriate safeguards must be documented, such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

## Security

WorkRH uses tenant scoping, role-based access, JWT authentication, internal service keys, production readiness checks, encrypted connector secrets, backups and operational monitoring.

## Data Subject Rights

Employees should primarily contact their employer for HR data requests. WorkRH assists customers with access, rectification, deletion, restriction and portability requests where applicable.
